Privacy Policy

Car Dashboard (Android, package com.carplay.dashboard) · Developer: Applicool · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Car Dashboard app (the "App") uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies only to this App. Our general privacy policy for all our apps is available at applicool.space/privacy.html.

In short

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own servers that collect your personal data.
  • Location is used only while the App is open, to show your speed, your position on the map and local weather. It is never collected in the background.
  • Your contacts are read only on your phone to show your contact list. They are never uploaded.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your advertising ID. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

1. Permissions and how they are used

Location (precise and approximate)

Used only while the App is open, to show your current speed, your position on the map and the weather where you are. Before Android asks for this permission, the App explains why it is needed. You can refuse it; the speedometer, map and weather will then not work. The App does not request background location.

To display the map and weather, the App sends requests to third-party services (see section 3). These requests include the map area you are viewing or your approximate coordinates, plus your IP address, as with any internet request. We do not store your location history.

Contacts

Used only to show your contacts inside the App so you can start a call with one tap. Contacts are read on your device and are never uploaded, stored by us or shared. Calls are placed through your phone's own dialer; the App does not make calls by itself.

Internet and network state

Used to load maps, weather, radio stations and ads.

Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock

Used so that radio keeps playing, with a media notification you control, when you switch apps or turn off the screen.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves a few settings locally on your phone: selected dashboard theme, speed unit, the car brand, model and year you selected, "keep screen on" preference, Premium status and simple usage counters (for example, how many times the App was opened, used to decide when to show a review request). This information stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

If you send feedback from the in-app rating window, it is not transmitted to any server.

4. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): the App shows Google's consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • No ads: Premium subscribers do not see ads.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect location in the background.
  • We do not upload your contacts or call history.
  • We do not require an account, name, email address or phone number.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because the App does not send your personal data to our own servers, we have no personal data of yours to keep or delete. Settings stored on your device are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Data processed by third parties (such as Google) is kept according to their own policies; you can manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.

7. Children

The App is intended for drivers and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

The App uses secure connections (HTTPS) for its own requests wherever the service supports it. Some radio stations only broadcast over plain HTTP; when you play such a station, the audio stream is not encrypted.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial laws such as Québec's Law 25, the GDPR in Europe, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, most requests are best handled through the settings described above or with the third-party service concerned. You can also contact us with any question.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
